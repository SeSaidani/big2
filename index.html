          Rules

          • 56 cards: a standard deck plus 4 jokers. Everyone gets 14 cards.
          • Ranks: 3 < 4 < … < K < A < 2. Suits break ties: ♦ < ♣ < ♥ < ♠.
          • The holder of 3♦ leads first, and that play must include the 3♦.
          • Play a single, pair, triple or five-card hand. Followers must play the same number of cards and beat it, or pass. Once you pass you're out until the trick ends.
          • You can always pass, even with the lead: the lead moves to the next player. If all four players pass the lead in a row, it's back with whoever had it first, and they must play. (The opening 3♦ play can't be passed.)
          • Last card: if the next player has one card left, any single you play must be your highest (any joker counts as highest). Pairs and five-card hands are unaffected.
          • Five-card hands, low to high: straight, flush, full house, four of a kind + kicker, straight flush, five of a kind.
          • A straight is worth its highest card, and the ace and 2 are low in the two wrap-around straights: A-2-3-4-5 (5-high) and 2-3-4-5-6 (6-high) are the weakest, then 3-4-5-6-7 up to 10-J-Q-K-A on top. J-Q-K-A-2 is not allowed. Equal straights are decided by the suit of the highest card.
          • Flushes compare highest card first, then the next, then suit.
          • Jokers are wild: each one becomes whatever card makes its hand strongest. A joker played alone beats any natural single, but jokers don't beat each other.
          • The real card beats the joker: if two plays would otherwise be equal, the one with more real cards wins. So 5♣ 5♠ beats 5♦ + joker, and real 2♣ 2♠ beats two jokers.
          • If nobody left in the trick can beat a play, they're all passed automatically and the player who made it leads again straight away.
          • The first player to empty their hand wins the round.
          • Online scoring: each loser pays the winner per card left in hand: 1 point a card for 1-7 cards, 2 for 8-10, 3 for 11-13, and 4 a card if they never played. Going out with a straight flush or five of a kind doubles every payment. Scores always add up to zero.
          • Online games: only one game runs at a time and an admin starts it. Once a game has started it belongs to the people seated until it finishes or an admin cancels it (a cancelled game is not scored). There is no turn timer: the game waits for anyone who is offline, and you can go back to the lobby and return to your seat at any time.
          • Chat is available while two or more people are at the table.
          • Texts: every account has a mobile number. When it becomes your turn and you are not looking at the game, you get one text with a link (never any cards), at most one every ten minutes. You can switch this off in the Phone dialog. Your number is only used for this, and only you and the server ever see it.

          Keys: Enter = play · P = pass · Esc = clear selection
